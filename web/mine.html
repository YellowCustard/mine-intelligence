What the vision layer is, and is not

carried in every event payload
Advisory, EMESRT Level 7 Every detection here warns a person. Nothing on this page actuates plant or a vehicle. Intervention at Level 8–9 needs functional-safety certification and OEM machine integration — a different product on a different timescale.
Proximity is a warning, not a control Person-near-plant raises an alarm and logs an evidence clip. It does not brake the excavator, and it does not replace the site's exclusion-zone procedure.
Truck fill is a proxy Load state is estimated from tray geometry, not weighed. It tracks the weightometer to ±4.1% over six weeks — useful for cycle analytics, not for a royalty calculation.
Dust derates everything Frame rate drops to 12 fps in poor visibility and the air purge takes each camera offline for about 40 s per cycle. Both are logged rather than hidden.
